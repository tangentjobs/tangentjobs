About Us

Founded 27 years ago in the UAE, Tangent Group is a trusted recruitment agency specializing in overseas and local manpower supply. Over the years, we have successfully deployed thousands of candidates and established ourselves as world leaders in providing innovative workforce solutions.

Our team provides strategic business consulting, application services, technology solutions, and management services. With extensive experience in executing HR and recruitment projects across the UAE and GCC, we continue to deliver exceptional results. Today, we manage over 35,000 employees across diverse industries.
