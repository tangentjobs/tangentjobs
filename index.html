About Us

Founded 27 years ago in the UAE, Tangent Group is a trusted recruitment agency specializing in overseas and local manpower supply. Over the years, we have successfully deployed thousands of candidates and established ourselves as world leaders in providing innovative workforce solutions. As a single-stop provider for all business-related needs, including HR & Recruitment, Tangent connects human potential with the power of business advisory for both clients and candidates. We proudly serve organizations of all sizes across various industry sectors through our specialized brands and maintain a worldwide network of offices across continents — “A global reach with a local touch.”

Our team provides strategic business consulting, application services, technology solutions, and management services. With extensive experience in executing HR and recruitment projects across the UAE and GCC countries including Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and Kuwait, we continue to deliver exceptional results. Today, we manage over 35,000 employees across diverse industries in the UAE. Our reputation is built on integrity, professionalism, and a strong global network of talent partners.
